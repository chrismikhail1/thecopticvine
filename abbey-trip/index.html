What to expect

A weekend to abide

Young adults from Houston, DFW, Austin, San Antonio and beyond spend a weekend living the rhythm of the monastery: praying with the fathers, serving with our hands, and making room to hear God.

Pray

Midnight Praises before dawn, two Divine Liturgies, Vespers, and Tamgeed each night with the monks of the Abbey.

Learn

Three lectures across the weekend, plus a guided reflection and a journal to carry the weekend home with you.

Serve

A morning of work alongside the monks, and a food donation drive to stock the monastery kitchen.

Weekend schedule

The rhythm of the Abbey

Early mornings, full days, quiet evenings. Times may shift a little once we're there.

    Set an alarm. Midnight Praises start at 4:00 AM on Saturday and 3:00 AM on Sunday.

    Before you go

    What to bring

    Pack

    • Bible and Agpeya
    • Modest church clothes for Liturgy and prayers
    • Comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes for the work session
    • Toiletries, towel and any medications you need
    • A light jacket. The church and early mornings can be cool
    • An alarm clock, so your phone can stay off

    Prepare

    • Check your room assignment before you arrive
    • Sign up for an item on the monastery food donation list
    • Fast before Saturday and Sunday Liturgy if you plan to take Communion
    • Talk with your Father of Confession before the trip
    • Plan to keep your phone away for most of the weekend
    • Arrive between 5:00 and 7:00 PM Friday for check-in
    Guided reflection

    “Though I was blind, now I see.”
